SNAP ABAWD screener

A short check of whether the DTA work rules apply to someone getting SNAP, or whether they may be exempt or had a good reason for missing hours. Two versions, styled for two different sites, ask the same questions and run the same decision logic. Answers stay on the visitor's device.
